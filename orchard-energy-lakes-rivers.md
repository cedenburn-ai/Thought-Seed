## The Mechanism

**On touch: the node receives the full deposit. Then half of that deposit is divided among its links, proportional to link strength. Each hop repeats the rule on what it received.**

Energy is created, not conserved. The shells sum geometrically — 1 + ½ + ¼ + ⅛ … — so every touch mints exactly **2× the deposit in total, regardless of graph topology**. That uniformity is the honest-money rule: a node's energy always means *attention received*, never attention-times-local-topology.

## The Analogy

The graph is a landscape of **lakes** (nodes) connected by **rivers** (links), living under **rainfall** (the user's attention).

A deposit is rain falling on one lake. The lake fills fully — the touched node is the primary beneficiary. The overflow drains outward through its rivers, and **the share each river carries is proportional to its width** relative to all the rivers leaving that lake. One river wide enough can carry 99% of the drainage. Each downstream lake keeps what arrives and passes half onward through its own rivers. The halo dies out geometrically; the watershed warms most where the rain fell.

Key correspondences:

- **River width = link strength.** Drainage divides at the *source* lake by the source's own outlets — which is why the same river can be 99% of a pond's drainage and 1% of a great lake's. Share is a property of the flow; width is a property of the wire.
- **Dry channels persist.** An unused river stops carrying water but stays carved into the landscape — no-delete. Only the *water* is rationed; the riverbed is topology, waiting. Resurrection (LogXform) is weather that rains on a dry watershed: the old channels immediately route the new water the way they routed the old.
- **The shape of the drainage IS the memory.** The watershed's accumulated form — carved by every flood that ever passed — is what the system knows.
- **New rivers are dug at the local average width** (link inserted at the node's link-local mean strength) — born mid-pack, instantly competitive. What starves is not the new but the *old unused*, buried by the local rising mean as siblings widen.

## Why Creating Energy Is Right

**1. Forgetting requires minting.** Nothing decays on a clock — forgetting is the rising mean, and the mean only rises if total energy grows. A conserved graph is zero-sum: the mean stays flat and nothing is ever forgotten. The inflation is not a side effect; it *is* the forgetting mechanism. Untouched nodes are savings devalued by the printing press, and every act of attention runs the press.

**2. It matches real minds.** Spreading activation in human memory is duplicative, not conservative — activating *doctor* primes *nurse* without deactivating *doctor*. Priming doesn't steal from the source.

**3. The system is open, and open systems are the living ones.** The energy isn't created from nothing — it enters from outside, from the user's attention, which is genuinely scarce and genuinely spent. Every mint is backed by real cognitive expenditure. **The user is the sun.** Conserved systems run to equilibrium, and equilibrium is heat death; structure only forms in systems held far from equilibrium by an external flux. The substrate is alive exactly in proportion to the attention feeding it.

## The One Rule Written in Stone

**Creation is policy; uneven minting is forgery.**

The danger was never inflation — it's *shape-dependent* minting. The pathological variant (retain 100% AND forward 100%) mints totals that scale with how many shells the impulse reaches, so sparse chain regions mint more per touch than dense ones, and energy stops meaning attention. The halving rule is the honest mint: **same total minted for every touch, everywhere, forever — 2× the deposit, topology-independent.**

Bonus: forwarding half sets the branching ratio to ½, so every cascade dies out on its own, riding on continuous injection — bounded cascades on an open flow. That is the critical regime (§7 of the retrieval spec) achieved *by construction* rather than by tuning.
